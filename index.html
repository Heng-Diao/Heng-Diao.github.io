Invention patents

Two granted Chinese invention patents, co-invented with Tingting Liu. The English overviews below explain the technical methods; the original Chinese patent documents are provided for reference.

01

A Method and System for Stance Annotation in English Texts

A hybrid annotation approach combining explicit linguistic rules with a trainable NLP model to identify stance markers in English corpora.

Granted 8 May 2026CN 121543587 BZL202610057958.7

Read English technical summary ↓

The patent describes a hybrid framework for identifying stance expressions in English texts at scale. It begins with part-of-speech annotation and linguistic rules covering modal and semi-modal verbs, stance adverbs, and more complex constructions involving stance verbs, adjectives, or nouns followed by to- and that-complement clauses. These rules are used to produce an annotated dataset, which in turn supports training a RoBERTa-Large-based token-classification model. The method links interpretable linguistic criteria with automated sequence labelling, providing a route to corpus-scale stance annotation and subsequent linguistic analysis. Its focus is the annotation workflow and the underlying language-sensitive decisions, rather than simply matching a predefined list of keywords.

Inventors: Tingting Liu and Heng Diao
Patent holder: Tianjin University of Finance and Economics

02

Intelligent Analysis and Text Optimisation for International Financial Discourse: A Method and System

A corpus-informed approach to analysing English financial texts through automated feature annotation, multidimensional profiling, and model-assisted revision.

Granted 8 May 2026CN 121543600 BZL202610071079.X

Read English technical summary ↓

The patent sets out a framework for analysing and refining English-language financial discourse. It covers collecting and cleaning financial news corpora, training models to annotate linguistic features, and establishing a reference profile using frequencies per 1,000 tokens and Multi-Dimensional Analysis. A target text can then be compared with these reference patterns through dimension scores and feature-frequency measures. The proposed system also incorporates a domain-adapted language model to offer analysis-informed suggestions for text revision. By grounding feedback in corpus evidence and quantitative linguistic features, the method connects computational discourse analysis with the improvement of financial communication aimed at international readers.

Inventors: Tingting Liu and Heng Diao
Patent holder: Tianjin University of Finance and Economics
